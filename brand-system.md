# DOERMADE Brand System — Working Direction

## Core idea
**Create More Doers.**

DOERMADE exists to help people move from *I want to do this* to *I did it.*

## Purpose
Help more people become capable, confident Doers.

## Mission
Make doing easier by connecting people with useful products, practical solutions, and clear know-how so they can build, repair, maintain, reuse, repurpose, and create with confidence.

## Vision
Build DOERMADE into a trusted, scalable company that helps people accomplish more themselves and uses profitable growth to create measurable value for customers, communities, and the environment.

## Brand promise
**From “I want to” to “I did.”**

DOERMADE should reduce friction between intention and accomplishment. Sometimes that means a product. Sometimes a kit. Sometimes an instruction, video, checklist, or resource.

## What DOERMADE is
A commerce and knowledge brand built around doing.

DOERMADE is not limited to one product category and is not defined only by kits. It can sell individual products, materials, supplies, organized kits, complementary solutions, and practical know-how across many maker and maintenance categories.

## What DOERMADE is not
- Not just a craft-kit company
- Not just a tool company
- Not just an environmental brand
- Not a marketplace full of random products
- Not a company that makes the customer feel dependent on experts

The customer — the Doer — remains the hero.

## The DOERMADE Standard
**Maintain → Repair → Reuse → Repurpose → Recycle → Dispose only when necessary.**

This is an operating philosophy, not a lecture. It should guide product selection, instruction, packaging, and impact decisions.

## Website role
DOERMADE.com is the company’s home and should perform four jobs:

1. **Brand** — explain who DOERMADE is, why it exists, and what it stands for.
2. **Commerce** — present real products, kits, and solutions as they become available.
3. **Projects & Learn** — host project pages, videos, written instructions, downloads, troubleshooting, and useful references. QR codes in physical products should point to permanent DOERMADE project pages.
4. **Impact** — eventually document measurable outcomes from reuse, repair, waste reduction, community work, giving, and education.

## Information architecture
Primary navigation:
- Shop
- Projects & Learn
- About
- Impact
- Contact

The homepage should remain concise and route visitors toward these areas rather than attempting to contain every company statement.

## Visual identity direction
### Primary identity
Use a strong DOERMADE wordmark as the main corporate identity. The existing relaxed “Doer” figure may remain as a secondary icon, mascot, community mark, or apparel element rather than carrying the entire corporate identity by itself.

### Palette
The redesign starts with a durable maker-oriented palette that can work on websites, packaging, labels, cartons, apparel, and physical spaces:

- **Carbon** — `#111512`
- **Canvas** — `#F2EDE2`
- **Forge Green** — `#264F3C`
- **Brass** — `#CFA646`
- **Steel** — `#6C746E`
- **White** — `#FFFFFF`

The visual system should feel capable, practical, premium, and broad enough for woodworking, automotive, sewing, art, repair, leatherwork, home projects, and future B2B operations.

## Voice
- Capable, not preachy
- Practical, not corporate
- Encouraging, not childish
- Clear, not jargon-heavy
- Confident, not boastful

## Homepage messaging hierarchy
1. **Create More Doers.**
2. Useful products. Practical solutions. Clear know-how.
3. Choose a path: Shop / Projects & Learn / About DOERMADE.
4. Show the breadth of “doing” visually.
5. Explain the DOERMADE approach briefly.
6. Present the DOERMADE Standard.
7. Invite people to explore products and projects as they are released.

## Product-page / project-page architecture
Each DOERMADE project or kit should eventually have a permanent URL such as:

`doermade.com/projects/dm-001`

A project page can contain:
- Instructional video
- Written steps
- Included materials
- Required tools
- Safety information
- Troubleshooting
- FAQs
- Downloads
- Replacement / refill components
- Related projects

Physical QR codes should point to the DOERMADE project page rather than directly to a third-party video host so the destination remains under company control.
