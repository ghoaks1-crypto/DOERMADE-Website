What DOERMADE is

A company built around capability.

We believe people do more when the right product, the right information and the right solution meet at the right time.

DOERMADE is an Indiana-based company being built to make practical products and solutions easier to find, understand and put to work.

Sometimes that means a single useful item. Sometimes it means complementary products organized into a better solution. The goal is the same: remove friction between a person and the thing they are trying to accomplish.

P

Useful products

Practical items, materials and components chosen for real-world use—not shelf filler.

S

Better solutions

Products organized together when doing so makes a job simpler, faster, clearer or more reliable.

K

Clear know-how

Useful guidance that helps people understand what they need, why it matters and how to put it to work.

The DOERMADE standard

Before something becomes waste, ask what it can become next.

The best outcome is not always replacement. We favor keeping useful products, materials and skills in circulation for as long as they still create value.

01Maintain
02Repair
03Reuse
04Repurpose
05Recycle
06Dispose only when necessary

As the business grows, DOERMADE intends to turn this standard into measurable action. Landfill reduction is one early impact area under consideration, with specific goals to be defined as operations develop.
