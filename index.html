What is DOERMADE?

DOERMADE is an Indiana-based retail and commerce company built around a simple idea: make doing easier.

Useful Products

Individual products, supplies and components selected for real-world use.

Practical Solutions

Complementary products organized together when doing so makes the job simpler, faster or more reliable.

Knowledge

Clear guidance that helps customers understand what they need and how to put it to work.

Our Purpose

To help more people become Doers — people with the confidence and resources to accomplish more themselves.

Mission

Make useful products, practical solutions and know-how easier to access while helping extend the useful life of the things people already own.

Vision

Build a trusted, scalable and financially strong company that creates value for customers and measurable good for communities and the environment.

Business Philosophy

Start lean, prove demand, build wealth responsibly and use success to fund work that leaves something better.

Use More. Waste Less.

Before something becomes waste, we believe there should be a better question: can it still be maintained, repaired, reused, repurposed or recovered?

Maintain → Repair → Reuse → Repurpose → Recycle → Dispose only when necessary

One early impact area under consideration is measurable landfill reduction. DOERMADE intends to develop specific, trackable impact goals as the business grows.

Reuse & Refurbishment

Future programs designed to keep useful products and materials in circulation longer.

B2B Services

Future kitting, packaging, wholesale, fulfillment and lifecycle support opportunities.
