The DOERMADE approach

Make doing easier. Not more complicated.

DOERMADE should reduce friction between a person and the thing they want to accomplish.

01

Start with the goal.

What is the Doer trying to build, fix, maintain, reuse or create?

02

Find what works.

Select useful products and compatible materials instead of adding clutter for its own sake.

03

Explain it clearly.

Use straightforward instructions, demonstrations and references when knowledge is the missing piece.

04

Help the Doer succeed.

The outcome matters more than the transaction. A successful project creates a more capable customer.
